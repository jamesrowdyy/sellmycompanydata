Questions

How much is the referral fee?

A flat $10,000 for every referred business that completes a licence. The amount is confirmed in writing before you make the introduction, so you always know the number up front.

When do I get paid?

Once the licence completes and the supplying company has been paid. The timing is set out in the written referral terms.

Do I need to work at the company?

No. Anyone can refer a business: a supplier, a customer, an advisor, an accountant, or a friend who runs one.

What if they say no?

Nothing happens. We reach out once, and if they're not interested we stop. No fee is owed and there's no cost to you either way.

Can I refer more than one business?

Yes, and it's encouraged. Send as many as you like through the form.

Do you contact them without my knowledge?

No. We confirm your referral terms with you first, then make one approach. You're told what happens at each stage.
