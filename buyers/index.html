Questions

Is the data synthetic?

No. Every record comes from a real company doing real work. We do not sell synthetic or model-generated filler.

How is it de-identified?

Names, contact details, credentials, payment details and other identifying fields are removed or replaced to a documented standard before delivery. The operational sequence stays intact, which is the part that carries the value.

What rights do we get?

A defined licence, not a transfer of ownership. Scope, permitted use, model types and duration are set out in writing and approved by the supplying company before anything ships. Non-exclusive by default.

Do you provide samples?

Yes. A redacted sample and a supply plan come before any licence is agreed, covered by an NDA, so you can judge quality first.

How fast can you deliver?

We reply to a brief within one business day. Typical delivery runs two to three weeks from an agreed scope, depending on sector and volume.

Do you work with data we can't scrape ourselves?

That's the point. This is the material that is not on the open web: internal decisions, exception handling and the record of how a task actually got finished.
